What the live product has

Checked 22 and 23 Nov on demo.jammie.ai, app.jammie.ai and staging

LiveThere today

  • Add your store, then Google sign-in before anything is read
  • “Opening your store”, “your first ideas are in”, then a wide first-idea card
  • Jammie pops in and the brand book flies in, with “email it to me”
  • Feed of cards: picture, video or carousel, with Keep and Let’s jam
  • Card menu: share, save, later, less like this, give feedback
  • Idea sheet: caption, sources, how we’ll know, where it goes
  • Let’s jam: ship as is, change first, more like this, make it with Sloosh
  • A sent idea becomes a row at the top of the feed
  • Stories: today, trends, popular ads, industry news, how to
  • Start something new: a note to the team, typed or spoken
  • Command centre: five columns of numbers, with reports
  • What we learned (Memory here), Connectors, You, and a bell

Docs onlyWritten down, not reachable

  • Three taps: only on a store nobody has read
  • “Here’s what we found”, “sit back” and “your first idea”: left out; the passport opens the feed
  • Onboarding’s side roads: not a store, a personal page, a failed read
  • Locked ideas and “unlock everything”: left out of the prototype
  • Your store: closed in production
  • “Your wrapped”, a monthly recap story: left out of the prototype
  • Inside the Shopify admin

AddedMine, not in the live product

  • One tall card fills the height, as on Winning Ads. Its words sit on the picture over a blur.
  • Jam is the red action at the foot of the action column, not a button on the card.
  • One top bar on every page: the bell and You sit in the same place and never move.
  • A panel (Why, Jam, a note, Updates, You) opens beside whatever page you are on.
  • The command centre is a deck of full-height columns. Saved, Memory and Connectors are one centred column.
  • A page’s own tabs sit on a second row under the top bar, never in it.
  • Saved: what you Kept and what you put aside with Later. Live keeps Liked closed.
  • Sign in is a popup: Google, or first name, last name and email. Live uses a full page with Google only.
  • A story plays full screen like an Instagram story: bars along the top, tap a side to move, and it moves on by itself.
  • Reading is one line that shimmers and changes. Then three taps, then a brand passport that arrives shut and opens.
  • Each of the three taps shows: Jammie changes look, and felt icons land beside it.
  • A note to the team has its box at the foot, with Send inside it.
  • Light and dark, switched from You.
  • Teammates are named in words only, on a pill on each card. Live draws nine characters; here only Jammie is drawn.
  • A popup on live is a side panel here, and a sheet on a phone: connecting a tool, a report.
  • Live’s settings (role, teammates, see the team grow) and its deck view (the same ideas grouped by teammate) are left out. A floating Demo button holds only light or dark and links to these pages.
  • All four demo stores open. Arata, Gully Labs and LEGO® India carry their profile, what Jammie read, their first ideas and the top ten cards of their live feed (pictures only; their videos are not copied). Their Command centre, reports and connector figures are Comet’s simulated ones.
  • Work lets you push a jam to its next stage yourself, so the board can be tried. On live the team moves it.
  • Report figures beyond the board’s own, the day-by-day numbers, and “what you keep” once costs are saved are worked out from the demo’s, not read.
  • A credits step for Sloosh. Live promises one; I did not open it.
  • The three taps’ questions are my guess.
